# Object Categories

BrainCoach uses three object categories:

## Reality Objects

Objects that represent reality.

* Event
* Observation
* Deviation
* Signal
* Phenomenon

---

## Knowledge Objects

Objects that represent understanding.

* Research
* Model
* Season
* Knowledge Object

---

## Operational Objects

Objects that support production and distribution.

* Insight Package
* Asset Blueprint
* Generated Asset
* Feedback Event

---

# Event

Definition:

Something that happened in reality.

Purpose:

Capture reality before interpretation.

Examples:

* School year ended
* University admissions started
* New AI model released

---

# Observation

Definition:

A factual observation about reality.

Purpose:

Describe what was observed without explanation.

Examples:

* Students forget material after exams
* Parents increasingly discuss AI tutors

---

# Deviation

Definition:

A meaningful difference between expected reality and observed reality.

Purpose:

Detect model error and identify potential change.

Examples:

Expected:

Parents discuss self-learning.

Actual:

Parents discuss AI.

---

# Signal

Definition:

A potentially important change, anomaly, trend or emerging pattern.

Purpose:

Identify developments that may require investigation.

Examples:

* Increased educational autonomy
* Growing AI-assisted learning

---

# Phenomenon

Definition:

A validated recurring human pattern.

Purpose:

Create stable objects for research and knowledge production.

Examples:

* Self-Learning Capability
* Dependency Learning
* External Motivation Collapse

---

# Research

Definition:

A structured investigation of a phenomenon.

Purpose:

Generate understanding.

Outputs:

* findings
* mechanisms
* hypotheses
* models

---

# Model

Definition:

A reusable explanatory structure.

Purpose:

Explain mechanisms and relationships.

Example:

Learnability

=

Curiosity × Autonomy × Environment

---

# Season

Definition:

A structured research program focused on a strategic topic.

Purpose:

Coordinate long-term research efforts.

Examples:

* Self-Learning Season
* Parent Decision Season
* AI Learning Season

---

# Knowledge Object

Definition:

A reusable unit of validated knowledge.

Purpose:

Provide standardized inputs for operations and automation.

Knowledge Objects may contain:

* research
* model
* phenomenon
* season

---

# Insight Package

Definition:

The output of the Extraction Module.

Purpose:

Convert knowledge into reusable meaning.

Structure:

* observation
* question
* tension
* paradox
* mechanism
* model

---

# Asset Blueprint

Definition:

A production specification for an asset.

Purpose:

Define packaging before generation.

Structure:

* asset_type
* goal
* angle
* structure

---

# Generated Asset

Definition:

A completed asset ready for distribution.

Examples:

* Reel
* Carousel
* Story
* Telegram Post
* Lesson
* Workshop
* Presentation

---

# Feedback Event

Definition:

A reaction generated after distribution.

Examples:

* comment
* share
* save
* DM
* consultation request
* interview response

Purpose:

Reconnect reality to knowledge.

---

# Butterfly Principle

Small deviations may become large phenomena.

The system should prefer registration over dismissal.

Weak signals should be preserved until their significance becomes clear.

---

# Object Governance Rule

Do not create new object types unless necessary.

Prefer extending existing objects.

Architecture Stability > Architecture Expansion.
