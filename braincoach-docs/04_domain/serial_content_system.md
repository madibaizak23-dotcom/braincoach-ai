## VISUAL WORLD PRINCIPLE
Core rule
The main character of BrainCoach is the phenomenon, not Malik.

Visual modes
Observation: strange situations, everyday life, people, behavior, real moments, contradictions.

Research: paper, pencil, notebooks, handwritten notes, diagrams, questions, unfinished models, traces of thinking.

Documentary: real people, school, sport, family, work, environment, everyday reality.

Malik on camera
Malik appears rarely and intentionally, only when the research needs an authorial presence: at the start of a season, on a major hypothesis, when inviting people into research, and after a series of observations.

Before every reel ask: what is strange here?
If nothing is strange, there is no phenomenon.
If there is no phenomenon, there is no BrainCoach.
