# Content Rule

Каждый материал должен отвечать минимум на один из вопросов:

* Что ограничивает развитие?
* Как формируется самостоятельность?
* Как появляется самообучаемость?
* Почему потенциал не превращается в результат?
* Какие механизмы стоят за изменениями?

# 2026-06-18 Market-Pain-First Update

Status: Active Constraint

Content should not start from the phenomenon when BrainCoach is testing an offer.

Content should start from market pain and then use the phenomenon diagnostically.

New formula:

```text
Market Pain
↓
Diagnostic Phenomenon
↓
Mechanism
↓
Next Step
```

Example:

```text
We invested so much. Why is there no result?
↓
Potential Exists - Results Do Not
↓
Trajectory Risk
↓
Interview / GPS / trajectory review
```

Visual OS remains active as the visual meaning system.

Its input changes to:

```text
Market Pain
↓
Phenomenon
↓
Signal
↓
Visual World
↓
Color Function
↓
CTA / Reflection
```

Conversion is treated as a learning signal:

* the market understands the model;
* the pain is formulated accurately;
* the next step has value;
* the offer hypothesis is closer to money.
