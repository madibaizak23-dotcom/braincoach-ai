# Parent Segments v1

Status: Active

Date: 2026-06-07

Purpose:

Identify parent segments experiencing the highest pain related to dependence on external management.

---

# Core Market Problem

Dependence on External Management

Definition:

The child requires continuous external initiation, supervision, prompting, regulation, or support in order to convert ability into results.

---

# Segment 01

Exhausted Manager Parent

Core Thought:

"I feel like my child's project manager."

Primary Phenomena:

* PH-001 Everything Understands But Doesn't Act
* PH-002 Doesn't Start Without Me

Pain:

* constant reminders
* daily supervision
* repeated explanations
* emotional exhaustion

Already Tried:

* motivation
* punishment
* rewards
* schedules

Success Looks Like:

* child starts independently
* reminders become rare
* fewer conflicts

Purchase Trigger:

"I am tired of managing everything."

Willingness To Pay:

High

---

# Segment 02

Tutor Dependency Parent

Core Thought:

"Progress disappears when support disappears."

Primary Phenomena:

* PH-003 Tutor Helps Only While Present

Pain:

* paying continuously
* no transfer of skills
* dependence on tutor

Already Tried:

* additional tutors
* more lessons
* more practice

Success Looks Like:

* child continues after tutor leaves
* results remain stable
* less dependence on external help

Purchase Trigger:

"We pay, but nothing sticks."

Willingness To Pay:

Very High

---

# Segment 03

Potential Anxiety Parent

Core Thought:

"My child is capable, but nothing happens."

Primary Phenomena:

* PH-004 Potential Exists But Results Do Not

Pain:

* fear of wasted potential
* uncertainty about future
* frustration with inconsistency

Already Tried:

* enrichment programs
* competitions
* tutoring

Success Looks Like:

* visible progress
* consistent execution
* increasing responsibility

Purchase Trigger:

"I don't want talent to be wasted."

Willingness To Pay:

High

---

# Segment 04

Future Risk Parent

Core Thought:

"What happens when I am no longer there?"

Primary Phenomena:

* PH-001
* PH-002
* PH-003
* PH-004

Pain:

* university readiness concerns
* life readiness concerns
* dependence concerns

Success Looks Like:

* self-directed behavior
* independent problem solving
* responsibility without supervision

Purchase Trigger:

"Will my child survive without constant guidance?"

Willingness To Pay:

Very High
