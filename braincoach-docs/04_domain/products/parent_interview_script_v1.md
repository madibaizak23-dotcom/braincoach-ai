# Parent Interview Script v1

Status: Active

Date: 2026-06-07

Purpose:

Validate the market problem of dependence on external management and identify observable proof of success that parents value enough to pay for.

---

# Interview Rules

Do not pitch.

Do not explain BrainCoach.

Do not teach.

Do not defend ideas.

Ask.

Listen.

Record exact language.

---

# Block 1 — Context

Tell me a little about your child.

How old is he/she?

What kind of student is he/she?

What are the strongest qualities?

What concerns you most right now?

---

# Block 2 — Current Reality

Let's talk about studying.

How does homework usually happen?

Who starts the process?

What happens if nobody reminds your child?

Can you describe what happened this week?

Walk me through a real example.

---

# Block 3 — Pain Discovery

What frustrates you most?

What situation repeats over and over again?

What conversations do you find yourself having repeatedly?

When do you feel most tired as a parent?

What makes you think:

"Why is this still happening?"

---

# Block 4 — Cost Of The Problem

What does this cost you?

Time?

Energy?

Arguments?

Stress?

Confidence?

What becomes impossible because of this problem?

How often does it happen?

Daily?

Weekly?

Occasionally?

---

# Block 5 — Previous Attempts

What have you already tried?

Tutors?

Rewards?

Punishments?

Schedules?

Monitoring?

Additional classes?

What worked temporarily?

What never worked?

---

# Block 6 — Proof Of Success

Imagine we meet again 30 days from now.

The problem is solved.

How would you know?

What would your child be doing differently?

What would you see?

What would stop happening?

What would happen more often?

How would a normal evening look?

---

# Block 7 — Observable Signals

Would any of these matter to you?

* starts without reminders
* finishes without supervision
* continues after difficulty
* asks better questions
* needs less control
* learns independently

Which of these would matter most?

Why?

---

# Block 8 — Emotional Outcome

If this problem disappeared, what would change for you personally?

What would you stop worrying about?

How would your relationship with your child change?

What would become easier?

---

# Block 9 — Purchase Trigger

At what point would you seriously look for help?

What would have to happen?

What would convince you that a solution is worth paying for?

Have you ever paid for help with this before?

Why?

---

# Block 10 — Closing

If you could change only one thing about your child's learning behavior tomorrow, what would it be?

Why that one thing?

---
