# Proof of Success Framework v1

Status: Draft

Date: 2026-06-07

---

# Purpose

Define observable signals that indicate reduced dependence on external management.

The framework translates abstract learning outcomes into measurable parent-visible behavior.

---

# Principle

Parents do not buy concepts.

Parents buy observable changes.

BrainCoach therefore evaluates success through behavior rather than explanation.

---

# Core Success Formula

Less External Management

↓

More Independent Execution

↓

Parent Relief

---

# Parent Success Signals

Level 1

Starts without reminders

Evidence:

Child begins work within 5 minutes of planned start time.

---

Level 2

Continues without supervision

Evidence:

Child works independently without parent presence.

---

Level 3

Recovers without rescue

Evidence:

Child attempts problem solving before asking for help.

---

Level 4

Completes without intervention

Evidence:

Tasks are finished without external prompting.

---

Level 5

Transfers independently

Evidence:

Independent behavior appears across subjects and environments.

---

# Parent Proof Statements

Observable phrases:

* He started by himself
* She didn't need reminders
* He kept going after getting stuck
* She finished without supervision
* The tutor was not needed
* I can step back and it still works

---

# BrainCoach Success Metric

Primary Indicator:

Reduction in External Management Dependency

Measured Through:

* reminder frequency
* supervision frequency
* rescue frequency
* initiation frequency
* completion frequency
* transfer frequency

---

# Strategic Rule

Self Learning Capability remains the research object.

Independent Execution becomes the market-visible proof of progress.

Parents buy proof.

Researchers study mechanisms.
