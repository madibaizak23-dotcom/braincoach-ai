# ADR-001: Adopt BrainCoach Knowledge OS v1.0

## Status
Accepted

## Date
2026-06-03

## Context
BrainCoach requires a stable operating architecture for knowledge management, research operations, governance, memory, and infrastructure before expanding into semantic retrieval, agent orchestration, or advanced AI capabilities.

The project has established the following foundational artifacts:
- Canon
- Ontology
- Phenomenon Registry
- Season Registry
- Social Intelligence
- Governance artifacts

A stable architecture is required to prevent uncontrolled growth of entities, folders, layers, and competing sources of truth.

## Decision
Adopt BrainCoach Knowledge OS v1.0 as the official operational architecture of the BrainCoach ecosystem.

The architecture consists of five layers:
- Knowledge
- Research Operations
- Governance
- User Memory
- Infrastructure

All future work must operate within this structure.

Architectural changes are permitted only through the ADR process.

## Consequences
- BrainCoach Knowledge OS v1.0 becomes the accepted architecture baseline.
- No new layers are introduced without an approved ADR.
- No new architectural entities are introduced without an approved ADR.
- Governance becomes mandatory for architectural evolution.
- Operational work has priority over architectural expansion.
- Future ideas are recorded as future candidates until justified by operational needs.
