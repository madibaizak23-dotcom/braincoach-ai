# Content Factory — Asset Design Module v1.0

Status: Active

Date: 2026-06-05

---

# Purpose

The Asset Design Module transforms an Insight Package into reusable asset blueprints.

The purpose of Asset Design is not to generate content.

The purpose of Asset Design is to determine how knowledge should be packaged.

---

# Position In Factory

Knowledge Object

↓

Intake

↓

Extraction

↓

↓

Generation

↓

Feedback

---

# Responsibility

The Asset Design Module:

* selects asset types
* selects distribution goals
* selects communication angles
* defines structures
* creates asset blueprints

The Asset Design Module does NOT:

* generate final content
* perform research
* rewrite knowledge
* change meaning

---

# Core Principle

Meaning remains constant.

Packaging changes.

# Communication Goals

## Attention

Purpose:

Stop scrolling.

Examples:

* Reels
* Short Video
* Hook Assets

---

## Understanding

Purpose:

Explain a mechanism.

Examples:

* Carousel
* Visual Breakdown
* Educational Slides

---

## Dialogue

Purpose:

Collect reactions and signals.

Examples:

* Stories
* Polls
* Questions

---

## Reach

Purpose:

Spread ideas.

Examples:

* Threads
* Short Posts
* Social Fragments

---

## Depth

Purpose:

Increase understanding.

Examples:

* Telegram Post
* Article
* Research Summary

---

## Memory

Purpose:

Preserve organizational knowledge.

Examples:

* Knowledge Note
* Registry Update
* Case Note

# Asset Types

Current supported assets:

* Reels
* Carousel
* Stories
* Threads
* Telegram Post
* Knowledge Note

Future assets may include:

* Lesson
* Workshop
* Exercise
* Worksheet
* Presentation
* Consultation Script
* Product Brief
* Offer Brief

The Factory must support expansion without changing its architecture.

# Design Rule

One Insight Package may produce many blueprints.

The same mechanism may be distributed through different formats.

Knowledge is reused.

Meaning is preserved.

---

# Success Metric

Asset Design succeeds when:

* knowledge remains unchanged
* asset goals are clear
* generation becomes predictable
* new asset types can be added without redesigning the Factory

---

# Operational Principle

Extraction reveals meaning.

Asset Design determines packaging.

Generation creates the final asset.
