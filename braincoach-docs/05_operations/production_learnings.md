## Learning 001

Title:

Write scripts in subtitle blocks

Observation:

Paragraph scripts require manual subtitle splitting.

Finding:

Short subtitle blocks dramatically reduce editing effort.

Bad:

Some children understand the material well but do not start without reminders.

Good:

Some children

understand the material

well

But without reminders

they never start

Impact:

* Faster editing
* Larger subtitles
* Better readability
* Better mobile experience

Status:

Validated

## Learning 002

Title:

Opening scenes are the production bottleneck

Observation:

Most editing time is spent searching for opening visuals.

Finding:

First 3–5 seconds consume disproportionate production effort.

Decision:

Build Starter Scene Library.

Examples:

* empty desk
* notebook
* exam hall
* sports training
* locker room
* parent waiting
* child thinking

Impact:

* Reduced search time
* Faster assembly
* More consistent visual language

Status:

Validated

## Learning 003

Title:

Documentary footage outperforms generic AI visuals

Observation:

AI visuals often feel synthetic and reduce credibility.

Finding:

Real footage creates stronger attention and trust.

Preferred sources:

* sports
* training
* competitions
* school life
* family situations
* everyday environments

Impact:

* Higher authenticity
* Higher trust
* Better retention

Status:

Observed

## Learning 004

Title:

Scripts must be production-ready

Observation:

Writing and editing are disconnected.

Finding:

Production becomes faster when script structure matches editing structure.

Required fields:

* timestamp
* subtitle
* visual
* prompt

Impact:

* Less interpretation
* Faster assembly
* Easier delegation

Status:

Validated

---

## Learning 005

Title:

Production is the bottleneck

Observation:

Ideas are abundant.

Production capacity is limited.

Finding:

Most systems optimize ideation.

Actual constraint is packaging and publishing.

Implication:

Automation priority:

Production Workflow

>

Idea Generation

Impact:

Higher throughput with the same team size

Status:

Validated
