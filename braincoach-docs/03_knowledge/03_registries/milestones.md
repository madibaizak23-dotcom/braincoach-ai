# Milestone 3

Title:
Research First Architecture

Date:
2026-06-05

Status:
Completed

Deliverables:

* Reality Filter v1.0
* Operational Philosophy v2.0
* Signal Dictionary v1.0
* Phenomenon Registry v2.0
* Season Registry v1.1
* Content Factory v1.0
* PostgreSQL Research Layer
* Research Database Schema
* Knowledge Objects Registry v2.0
* Research OS Foundation
* Marketing Intelligence Architecture v1
* BrainCoach Newsroom v1

Key Decisions:

* Event separated from Signal
* Signal separated from Phenomenon
* Research Flow formalized
* Delivery Flow formalized
* Intelligence introduced as a first-class object class
* Research Layer introduced into PostgreSQL architecture
* Content becomes an output of knowledge production rather than the source of knowledge

Research Flow:

Reality

↓

Event

↓

Observation

↓

Signal

↓

Phenomenon

↓

Research

↓

Knowledge

Delivery Flow:

Knowledge

↓

Content Factory

↓

Channels

↓

Audience

↓

Feedback

↓

Reality

Outcome:

BrainCoach transitioned from a content-driven architecture to a research-driven architecture.

The system now distinguishes:

* Events
* Observations
* Signals
* Phenomena
* Knowledge

Content production is no longer the primary activity.

Research and knowledge production became the primary activity.

The Content Factory now operates as a distribution layer built on top of validated research.

Strategic Impact:

BrainCoach is no longer organized around topics.

BrainCoach is organized around human phenomena.

The core unit of discovery became:

Observation → Signal → Phenomenon

instead of:

Topic → Content

This establishes the foundation for:

* Marketing Intelligence
* Research Memory
* Phenomenon Tracking
* Multi-channel Content Factory
* Long-term Knowledge Compounding
* Research-driven Growth System
