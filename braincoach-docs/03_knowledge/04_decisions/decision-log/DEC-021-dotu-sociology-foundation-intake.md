# DEC-021 - DOTU And Sociology Foundation Intake

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-06-18

---

## Context

BrainCoach will use DOTU and Sociology source texts as foundation material for future ontology, knowledge objects, GPS interpretation, domain models, and marketing translation.

The canon remains unchanged.

The risk is not the source material itself.

The risk is uncontrolled movement from source terminology directly into accepted ontology or public marketing without defining meaning, measure, and usage boundaries.

## Decision

BrainCoach adopts a top-down source intake process:

```text
Foundation
↓
Ontology Candidates
↓
Knowledge / Intelligence Objects
↓
Signal and Phenomenon Mapping
↓
Domain Models
↓
Marketing Translation
↓
Operations / GPS / Survey / Offer
```

DOTU and Sociology concepts must first be processed as foundation material.

Only after foundation meaning is clarified may they become ontology candidates.

## Core Rule

The model is not the world.

The model is a map with a measure of approximation.

Terms describe processes, layers, regimes, and mechanisms.

Terms must not become labels for people.

Agents must not replace observed facts, закономерности, logic, measure, or feedback from reality with mass opinion or statistically common AI text.

Mass opinion may be used as a market signal.

Mass opinion is not proof of reality.

## Initial Concept Areas

The first intake cycle should cover:

* measure / мера;
* control / управление;
* full function of control / полная функция управления;
* psyche / психика;
* psyche structure / строй психики;
* biological automatic layer;
* fight / flight and automatic defensive reactions;
* herd-pack behavior / стадно-стайное поведение;
* culture / культура;
* society / общество;
* humaneness / человечность;
* conscience / совесть;
* morality / нравственность;
* crowd-elitism / толпо-элитаризм;
* social control and social inheritance.

## Consequences

No source concept moves directly into accepted ontology.

No source concept moves directly into marketing.

Every concept requires:

* source meaning;
* BrainCoach foundation meaning;
* measure of approximation;
* what it describes;
* what it does not describe;
* observable signals;
* allowed use;
* forbidden use;
* related BrainCoach objects.

## Non-Decision

This decision does not change canon.

This decision does not accept new ontology terms yet.

This decision does not create SQL, tables, embeddings, or automation.

This decision does not import full books into the repository.
