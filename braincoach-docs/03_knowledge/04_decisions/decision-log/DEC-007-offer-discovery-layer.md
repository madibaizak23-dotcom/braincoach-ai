# DEC-007 — Offer Discovery Layer

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-06-07

---

# Context

Stage 2 — Full Repository Registry has been completed.

BrainCoach now possesses:

* Research Architecture
* Knowledge Architecture
* Automation Architecture
* Repository Registry

The system can reliably transform:

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Signal

↓

Phenomenon

↓

Research

↓

Knowledge

However, a critical gap was identified.

The path from Knowledge to Revenue is not formalized.

Knowledge Objects do not directly generate business outcomes.

A translation layer is required between Knowledge and Products.

---

# Problem

Research produces:

* observations
* signals
* phenomena
* mechanisms
* knowledge assets

Business requires:

* offers
* products
* services
* revenue

No formal process exists for transforming validated phenomena into market-facing offers.

---

# Decision

Introduce Offer Discovery as a formal layer of the BrainCoach operating system.

New flow:

Reality

↓

Research

↓

Knowledge

↓

Offer Discovery

↓

Products

↓

Revenue

---

# Principles

Principle 1

Products must emerge from validated phenomena.

Products should not be invented independently from research.

---

Principle 2

Parent outcomes are primary.

Customers do not buy research.

Customers buy outcomes.

---

Principle 3

Every major phenomenon should map to:

Phenomenon

↓

Parent Pain

↓

Desired Outcome

↓

Offer

↓

Product

---

Principle 4

Offer Discovery is a knowledge activity before it is a marketing activity.

The objective is not persuasion.

The objective is translation.

---

# Initial Scope

The first Offer Discovery cycle will focus on:

SELF_LEARNING_CAPABILITY

and the core validated phenomena:

* Everything Understands But Doesn't Act
* Doesn't Start Without Me
* Tutor Helps Only While Present
* Potential Exists But Results Do Not

---

# Expected Outputs

Offer Discovery should produce:

* Offer Registry
* Product Registry
* Outcome Registry Extensions
* Phenomenon → Product Mapping

---

# Consequences

Research becomes the upstream source of product development.

Product creation becomes evidence-driven.

BrainCoach transitions from:

Research OS

↓

Knowledge OS

to

Research OS

↓

Knowledge OS

↓

Offer Discovery OS

↓

Business OS

---

Accepted
