# DEC-019 - Payment Trigger Discovery Priority

Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-06-18

---

## Context

BrainCoach already has active layers for research, knowledge, content production, visual meaning, automation, and GPS capture.

The current commercial bottleneck is not the ability to produce more content or define more phenomena.

The unresolved constraint is whether BrainCoach can identify the specific transformation parents are ready to pay for.

Current constraint:

```text
Phenomenon
↓
Payment Trigger
↓
Offer
```

## Problem

The system can describe phenomena such as dependence on external management, potential not converting into results, and tutor support not transferring into durable capability.

However, a phenomenon is not yet an offer.

Parents do not pay for the internal elegance of the model.

Parents pay when a painful, costly, time-sensitive transformation becomes clear.

## Decision

BrainCoach adopts Payment Trigger Discovery as the immediate priority for the next Offer Discovery cycle.

The Wednesday masterclass should be treated as a cold-audience offer discovery event, not only as a presentation.

The goal is to collect 10-15 high-quality payment stories:

* what parents already bought;
* where money was spent;
* what failed;
* what produced results;
* what became the last straw;
* what fear created urgency;
* what parents wanted to see before the final failure.

Clarification SJ-2026-06-18-002:

Self Learning Capability is not the buyable result.

It is the mechanism that helps produce the buyable result.

The current buyable result hypothesis is:

```text
trajectory stability
without constant external management
```

## Consequences

### Offer Discovery

Offer Discovery must move from phenomenon translation toward payment-story capture.

The primary question becomes:

```text
What transformation are parents already trying to buy?
```

Current answer to test:

```text
Trajectory stability without constant external management.
```

### Market Discovery

The strongest current market frame remains:

```text
We invested so much. Why didn't it work?
```

This phrase should be tested through concrete purchase history, not only resonance or agreement.

### Summer Map

Summer Map should evolve from a separate summer questionnaire into a universal BrainCoach Discovery Engine.

The engine should support different survey templates, Russian / Kazakh language selection, PostgreSQL-loaded questions, and answer classification as market, offer, and phenomenon signals.

### GPS

GPS should not be positioned primarily as a tracker.

It should be positioned as an early visibility instrument for understanding whether a child's trajectory investment is converting into real progress.

Working formulation:

```text
How can parents understand whether their investment in a child is turning into real progress?
```

### Content

Content must start from market pain, then move to diagnostic phenomenon, mechanism, and next step.

New formula:

```text
Market Pain
↓
Diagnostic Phenomenon
↓
Mechanism
↓
Next Step
```

Visual OS remains active, but it should receive market pain as the first input before selecting phenomenon, signal, visual world, and color function.

## Non-Decision

This decision does not approve a new database migration.

This decision does not create new PostgreSQL tables.

This decision does not replace DEC-007 Offer Discovery Layer.

This decision narrows the next Offer Discovery cycle toward payment-trigger validation.
