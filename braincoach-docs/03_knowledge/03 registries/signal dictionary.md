# Purpose

Signal Dictionary stores observable indicators of change in people, learning, technology, behavior, and society.

Signals are not explanations.

Signals are not phenomena.

Signals are early indicators that something important may be changing.

The purpose of Signal Dictionary is to help BrainCoach detect emerging phenomena before they become obvious.

---

# Core Principle

Events happen constantly.

Most events are noise.

Some events contain observations.

Some observations contain signals.

Some signals reveal phenomena.

# Definitions

## Event

Something that happened in reality.

Example:

* ENT started
* School year ended
* New AI model released
* Hockey championship finished

---

## Observation

Something noticeable within the event.

Example:

Parents search for control strategies more often than learning strategies.

---

## Signal

An observation that may indicate a deeper shift, pattern, contradiction, or emerging phenomenon.

Example:

Growing dependence of learning on external control.

---

## Phenomenon

A validated and recurring human pattern discovered through signal investigation.

Example:

Self-learning does not emerge automatically after years of external management.

---

# Signal Structure

Every signal must contain:

* SG-ID
* Title
* Source Event
* Observation
* Description
* Signal Type
* Potential Question
* Potential Phenomenon
* Strength
* Domains
* First Seen
* Status
* Notes

---

# Fields

## SG-ID

Unique signal identifier.

Example:

SG-001

---

## Title

Short signal name.

---

## Source Event

Event that generated the observation.

Example:

ENT 2026

---

## Observation

Observed fact without interpretation.

What exactly was noticed?

---

## Description

Why the observation may matter.

---

## Signal Type

Category of signal.

Allowed values:

* Behavioral
* Educational
* Cognitive
* Attention
* Social
* Technological
* Parenting
* Performance
* Economic

---

## Potential Question

Research question generated by the signal.

Example:

Why do students lose learning initiative when external control disappears?

---

## Potential Phenomenon

Phenomenon that may emerge if the signal proves persistent.

---

## Strength

Current importance level.

Allowed values:

* weak
* medium
* strong
* critical

---

## Domains

Where the signal appears.

Examples:

* learning
* school
* sport
* family
* entrepreneurship
* technology
* career

---

## First Seen

Date when the signal was first recorded.

---

## Status

Allowed values:

* candidate
* tracked
* validated
* linked_to_phenomenon
* archived

---

## Notes

Additional observations and context.

---

# Signal Evaluation Rules

A strong signal usually has one or more of the following characteristics:

* Unexpected
* Repeating
* Growing
* Contradictory
* Emotionally charged
* Appears across multiple contexts
* Challenges existing assumptions

---

# Signal Strength Framework

## Weak

Single observation.

No evidence of repetition.

---

## Medium

Repeated several times.

Pattern may be forming.

---

## Strong

Appears across multiple situations.

Worth investigation.

---

## Critical

May indicate a new phenomenon or major environmental shift.

Requires immediate research attention.

---

# Promotion Rule

Not every signal becomes a phenomenon.

However:

Every phenomenon must be linked to at least one signal.

# Strategic Value

Signal Dictionary is the early warning system of BrainCoach.

It allows the system to:

* detect change early
* identify emerging trends
* discover new phenomena
* generate research directions
* feed content production with reality

The goal is not to collect signals.

The goal is to discover what reality is trying to reveal.
