## Current Intelligence Thesis

BrainCoach GPS may require a trajectory intelligence layer that interprets human development through:

- desired state
- current state
- trajectory gap
- resonance
- dissonance
- trajectory filters
- cross-domain transfer
- lifecycle transition markers

This thesis is not accepted ontology. It is a working synthesis for further research and GPS product design.

## Relationship To Existing Intelligence

### Self Learning Capability

Self Learning Capability remains the central intelligence object.

GPS trajectory intelligence should not replace it. It may describe how Self Learning Capability appears over time as movement from external management toward self-directed development.

### External Management Dependency

The trajectory lens may explain dependence on external management as a persistent gap between desired independence and actual external control.

### Transfer Learning

The Unified Trajectory Model is an extension of Transfer Learning. The question is whether the same development mechanism can be detected across learning, sport, career, relationships, and self-development.

### Constraint Management

Personal boundaries as a trajectory filter may become a sub-mechanism of Constraint Management, or it may become a separate GPS mechanism if validated.

## Intelligence Assessment

The five GPS hypotheses are best understood as an emerging trajectory layer rather than five isolated objects.

Current assessment:

- GPS-HYP-001 and GPS-HYP-002 form the state model: alignment, mismatch, and gap.
- GPS-HYP-003 proposes a control/filtering mechanism.
- GPS-HYP-004 proposes cross-domain generalization.
- GPS-HYP-005 proposes timing and lifecycle transition markers.

This makes the natural next intelligence question:

Can GPS model development as movement across time from current state toward desired state, mediated by filters, constraints, signals, and domain transfer?

## Risks

### Premature Ontology

The terms are useful, but they are not yet stable enough for accepted ontology.

### Duplicate Terms

Resonance/dissonance may duplicate Deviation or Contradiction unless a GPS-specific distinction is defined.

### Numerological Drift

The Fibonacci lifecycle hypothesis must be grounded in observations and cannot become a diagnostic rule.

### Overgeneralization

The Unified Trajectory Model must demonstrate mechanism-level transfer, not surface analogy.
