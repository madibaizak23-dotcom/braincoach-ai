## Draft Research Objects

### GPS-HYP-001 - Resonance vs Dissonance

Status: draft

Hypothesis:

Resonance emerges when a person's desired trajectory matches their actual reality.

Dissonance emerges when there is a persistent gap between desired and actual state.

Initial classification: extension of existing deviation and trajectory concepts.

Not promoted.

### GPS-HYP-002 - Desired State to Current State Gap

Status: draft

Hypothesis:

Development can be viewed as reducing the gap between desired and actual state.

Initial classification: extension of existing case registry and external management dependency model.

Not promoted.

### GPS-HYP-003 - Personal Boundaries as Trajectory Filter

Status: draft

Hypothesis:

Personal boundaries function as a trajectory filter.

They:

- retain what is desired
- block what is undesired

Initial classification: likely new research object, related to Constraint Management, Own Goal, Direction Matters, and trajectory assessment.

Not promoted.

### GPS-HYP-004 - Unified Trajectory Model

Status: draft

Hypothesis:

The same development mechanisms can appear in:

- hockey
- career
- relationships
- learning
- self-development

Initial classification: extension of existing Self Learning Capability, Transfer Learning, and cross-domain case evidence.

Not promoted.

### GPS-HYP-005 - Fibonacci Lifecycle Hypothesis

Status: draft

Age nodes:

- 3
- 5
- 8
- 13
- 21
- 34
- 54
- 89
- 144

Key question:

Do natural human development transition points correspond to these stages?

Initial classification: partial duplicate / extension of existing age development markers in BrainCoach Ontology. The existing ontology uses 55, not 54, and does not include 144 as a human lifecycle node.

Not promoted.
