## GPS-HYP-001 - Resonance vs Dissonance

Status: draft

Hypothesis:

Resonance emerges when a person's desired trajectory matches their actual reality.

Dissonance emerges when there is a persistent gap between desired and actual state.

Already Exists:

Partial. No exact accepted ontology object named Resonance or Dissonance was found. Existing related structures include Deviation, Contradiction, expected-vs-actual feedback, and trajectory assessment.

Related Objects:

- Deviation
- Contradiction
- Feedback Module
- trajectory_assessment_v1
- BKOS reality-to-knowledge loop

Candidate Location:

- research
- gps
- ontology candidate, later

Recommendation:

extend

Validation Questions:

- What observable evidence indicates resonance?
- What observable evidence indicates dissonance?
- Is dissonance the same as contradiction, or is it a GPS-specific trajectory state?
- Can resonance/dissonance be detected through repeated tracker observations?

Promotion Conditions:

- At least three related observations or signal candidates.
- Clear distinction from Deviation and Contradiction.
- Decision Log entry before ontology promotion.

Status: draft

Personal boundaries function as a trajectory filter.

- retain what is desired
- block what is undesired

Already Exists:

No exact object found. Existing "boundaries" are mostly system/domain boundaries, not personal development mechanisms.

Related Objects:

- Constraint Management
- Self Regulation
- Own Goal
- Borrowed Goal
- Direction Matters
- Loss of Measure
- External Management Dependency
- trajectory_assessment_v1

Candidate Location:

- research
- ontology candidate
- gps

Recommendation:

create_new as draft only

Validation Questions:

- What behavior shows that a boundary retains a desired trajectory?
- What behavior shows that a boundary blocks an undesired trajectory?
