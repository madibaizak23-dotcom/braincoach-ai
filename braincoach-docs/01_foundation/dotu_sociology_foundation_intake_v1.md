## Purpose

BrainCoach needs DOTU and Sociology as foundation sources for thinking about:

* control;
* self-control;
* psyche;
* automatic biological layers;
* culture;
* society;
* development;
* education;
* marketing;
* offer discovery.

The purpose is not to copy books into the repository.

The purpose is to build a disciplined foundation layer that can later support ontology, knowledge objects, research questions, GPS interpretation, and market language.

## Core Boundary

Canon remains unchanged.

Foundation may be extended through source-based principles and interpretation rules.

Ontology may receive candidates only after foundation meaning is clarified.

Marketing may use derived customer language only after ontology and usage boundaries are defined.

## Model Rule

The model is not the world.

The model is a map with a measure of approximation.

Any category imported from DOTU or Sociology must be treated as:

```text
map
not territory
```

and as:

```text
description of a process / layer / regime
not a label for a person
```

## Agent Interpolation Rule

Large AI models may interpolate between source concepts, BrainCoach foundation principles, and observed reality.

Interpolation is allowed when it preserves the logic of the source and the BrainCoach layer boundary.

Interpolation is not canon.

Interpolation is not accepted ontology.

Interpolation is a hypothesis until it is checked against reality.

Every interpolated statement must remain traceable to:

* source concept;
* BrainCoach foundation meaning;
* observed signal or expected observable signal;
* decision or experiment it affects;
* feedback that can confirm, weaken, or correct it.

If an agent fills a gap by reasoning, it must mark the result as:

```text
inference
not source statement
not validated knowledge
```

## Anti-Noise Epistemic Hierarchy

Agents must not treat mass opinion, common phrasing, internet consensus, or statistically frequent text patterns as truth.

Mass opinion may indicate:

* a cultural script;
* a common belief;
* market language;
* an objection;
* a social signal.

Mass opinion does not by itself establish reality.

BrainCoach uses the following hierarchy when reasoning:

```text
1. Reality / observed fact
2. Repeated закономерность across contexts
3. Logical and mathematical coherence
4. Source-grounded model with stated measure
5. Experiment / outcome feedback
6. Expert interpretation
7. Market language / mass opinion
8. AI-generated synthesis
```

One fact can falsify many opinions.

One stable закономерность can outweigh millions of repeated claims.

Agents must therefore reduce AI-noise by asking:

1. Is this an observed fact or only a common statement?
2. Is there a закономерность, or only repetition?
3. Does the inference preserve measure and logic?
4. What would disconfirm this?
5. What feedback from reality is required?

When mass opinion is useful, label it as:

```text
market signal
not reality proof
```

## Reality Correspondence Rule

The goal is not to preserve a closed theory.

The goal is to make BrainCoach representations of reality correspond to reality more accurately over time.

Every foundation model must remain open to correction by:

* observation;
* repeated signals;
* failed predictions;
* contradictions;
* parent language;
* child behavior;
* market response;
* longitudinal outcomes.

The direction of work:

```text
model
↓
observation
↓
signal
↓
prediction / expectation
↓
feedback from reality
↓
model correction
```

The model improves when it helps BrainCoach see earlier, predict better, decide better, and correct faster.

## Biological-Automatic Layer Rule

Human development does not begin from a blank slate.

The human body and nervous system contain evolutionarily inherited automatic layers.

Examples:

* fight / flight reactions;
* automatic defensive responses;
* instinctive drives;
* group and hierarchy behavior;
* herd-pack dynamics;
* imitation;
* status sensitivity;
* environmental threat detection.

These layers are not moral labels.

They are inherited automatic control loops that culture, education, self-control, and conscious practice can reorganize, subordinate, compensate, or redirect.

BrainCoach should treat them as part of the human control system.

## Usage Boundary

Allowed:

* use concepts to describe systems, control loops, regimes, layers, and mechanisms;
* use concepts to generate research questions;
* use concepts to interpret signals carefully;
* use concepts to design GPS observation categories;
* use concepts to improve marketing depth after translation into parent language.

Forbidden:

* use concepts as labels for a person;
* use concepts as insults or status markers;
* use source terminology directly in public marketing without translation;
* present foundation concepts as validated BrainCoach phenomena before evidence;
* collapse biological automatic layers into moral judgment;
* collapse culture or society into a single cause.

## Agent Orientation Summary

For agents, this document is enough to orient the direction of reasoning if they follow these rules:

1. Do not change canon.
2. Start from foundation meaning.
3. Treat source concepts as models with measure, not reality itself.
4. Interpolate logically, but mark interpolation as inference.
5. Do not replace logic and evidence with mass opinion.
6. Treat mass opinion as a possible market signal, not proof of reality.
7. Convert inference into ontology candidate only after usage boundaries are clear.
8. Convert ontology into marketing only through translation, never by direct source terminology.
9. Always ask what would be observed in reality if the model is accurate.
10. Correct the model when reality contradicts it.
