# BrainCoach Visual World Principle v1.0

Status: Draft
Date: 2026-06-04

## Purpose

This document defines who and what becomes the visual center of BrainCoach.

Its purpose is to prevent the brand from drifting into expert-centered content, influencer behavior, or motivational performance.

BrainCoach does not build attention around a personality.

BrainCoach builds attention around phenomena.

---

## Core Principle

The main character of BrainCoach is not Malik.

The main character is the phenomenon.

People do not return because they want to watch Malik.

People return because they want to understand something important about themselves and other people.

---

## Role of Malik

Malik appears rarely and intentionally.

He enters the frame only when the research requires an authorial presence.

Typical cases:

* Start of a research season
* Introduction of a major hypothesis
* Invitation into an investigation
* Reflection after a series of observations
* Important research conclusions

Malik is a guide inside the research world.

Not the center of it.

---

## Default Visual Modes

### Observation Mode

Used when showing a phenomenon.

Visual elements:

* strange situations
* everyday life
* people
* behavior
* real moments
* contradictions
* observable reality

Question:

What is happening here?

---

### Research Mode

Used when exploring a phenomenon.

Visual elements:

* paper
* pencil
* notebooks
* handwritten notes
* diagrams
* questions
* unfinished models
* traces of thinking

Question:

How might this work?

---

### Documentary Mode

Used when showing context.

Visual elements:

* real people
* school
* sport
* family
* work
* environment
* everyday reality

Question:

Where does this phenomenon appear?

---

## Visual Compass

Before creating any reel ask:

What is strange here?

If nothing is strange,
there is no phenomenon.

If there is no phenomenon,
there is no BrainCoach.

---

## Character Principle

BrainCoach does not position Malik as:

* an expert from above
* a motivational speaker
* a guru
* a personal brand celebrity

Malik is:

* observer
* investigator
* researcher
* companion in inquiry

His role is simple:

to notice strange things in human behavior
and invite others to investigate them together.

---

## World Building Rule

The visual world should feel larger than the author.

The audience should gradually recognize:

* notebooks
* handwritten questions
* research diagrams
* paper
* pencil
* observations
* contradictions
* cognitive experiments
* real life situations

These elements are recurring characters of the BrainCoach world.

---

## Final Principle

People may arrive because of a reel.

They stay because of the world.

The world exists to investigate one thing:

the human being.
